About Bright Side

A few years ago, I was struggling. An eating disorder. Depression. Anxiety. All at once. Most days, my mind worked against me.

Not much helped. But one small thing did: word searches.

Scanning a grid, letter by letter, hunting for one word. It quieted my head like almost nothing else could. It didn't fix anything. It just gave me a few minutes where my attention had somewhere safe to go, instead of spiraling.

Bright Side grew out of that. Every puzzle is built around something gentle: kindness, nature, cozy mornings, small wins. Each one comes with a short note on why puzzles like this can actually help your mind settle. It's free. It always will be. Built for anyone who needs five quiet minutes today.

If you're going through something similar

It's real, and it's not permanent. A word search isn't a substitute for real support. But if you need somewhere to start:

  • National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988, day or night
